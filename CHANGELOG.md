# Changelog

## 1.3.1

### Changes

- AppImage now starts on X11 desktops without extra packages (fixes Linux Mint)

## 1.3.0

### Changes

- Controller support (Steam Deck and other gamepads), including name entry on the pad
- 7 new power-ups: 1UP extra life, kill -9 bombs, grep laser, ssh homing shots, SIGSTOP freeze, 2x score and rm -rf nuke
- Power-ups now last 30 seconds and SIGSTOP freezes enemies for 10 seconds
- Windows Update boss attacks less often
- AppImage for other distros with in-game updates from the main menu

## 1.1.0

### Changes

- 12 unique bosses, each with its own attack and theme music
- Endless fusion bosses from level 39 on
- 40 enemy types that unlock as you progress
- Quiet 80s chiptune music with its own toggle (N)
- Renamed to WinVaders
